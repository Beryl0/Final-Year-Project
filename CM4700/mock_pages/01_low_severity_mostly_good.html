Check your household bin collection days

Household waste and recycling services normally follow a regular collection pattern. Residents may need to check their bin days, place bins out by the correct time, and use the appropriate container for each type of waste.

Blue-lid, orange-lid and black refuse bins are used for different materials, while food caddies and battery bags may be collected separately. Collection reminders can help residents avoid missed collections.

Request bins and report damaged bins

Residents may need to request new bins, additional recycling capacity, food waste caddies or replacement equipment. Damaged bins should be reported so the council can decide whether they should be repaired or replaced.

Recycling centres and household waste

Recycling centres provide a way for households to dispose of items that cannot be placed in normal kerbside bins. Booking rules, accepted materials and site arrangements can vary depending on location and vehicle type.

Residents may also use recycling points for specific materials such as glass or textiles, depending on what is available locally.

More

You can also report a missed collection, request additional recycling capacity, check service disruptions, or find information about waste that needs special handling.

Coastal recycling and litter prevention

Visitors and residents are encouraged to use bins and recycling points responsibly. Reducing litter helps protect beaches, dunes and local wildlife.

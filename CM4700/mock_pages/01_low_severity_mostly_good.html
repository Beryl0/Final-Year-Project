Household bin collections and recycling

This mock page is based on common council service content. It includes mostly meaningful headings and relevant alt text.

Check your household bin collection days

Residents can check when their blue-lid, orange-lid and black refuse bins are due to be collected. Collections normally take place on the same day of the week, with each bin collected on a rotating schedule.

Recycling centres and household waste

Recycling centres allow residents to dispose of materials that cannot be placed in household bins. Booking requirements, accepted items and opening times may vary by site.

More

You can also request an additional recycling bin, report a missed collection or check current service disruptions.

Coastal recycling and litter prevention

Visitors and residents are encouraged to use recycling points and litter bins to help protect local beaches and coastal environments.

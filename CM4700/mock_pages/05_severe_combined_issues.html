Council services test page with combined accessibility issues

This mock page deliberately combines several types of poor alt text and weak headings. It is not intended to represent a good public website page. It is designed to stress-test the proof-of-concept AI modules.

The content refers to several council service areas so the page contains a mixture of waste, roads, schools, housing and leisure topics.

Schools, learning and community facilities

This mock page deliberately includes images with missing or misleading alternative text. The content is based on common school, learning and leisure service themes.

The purpose is to show that an image can fail because the alt attribute is missing, but it can also be problematic when alt text exists and is unrelated to the image.

School activities and pupil wellbeing

School activities can support pupils to build confidence, learn practical skills and take part in wider community projects. Schools may also provide information about meals, term dates, transport changes and support for families.

This image deliberately has no alt attribute, which should be treated as a high-severity rule-based finding if the image is informative.

Outdoor play spaces for children

Outdoor play areas give children opportunities to be active, socialise and develop confidence. Play spaces can also support health and wellbeing when they are safe, inclusive and maintained appropriately.

The image below deliberately uses misleading alt text. The alt text refers to a Council Tax payment form even though the image is intended to show children using play equipment.

Libraries, sport and cultural services

Leisure and cultural services can include sport and physical activity, libraries, museums, arts, community halls and aquarium services. These services support learning, wellbeing and community participation.

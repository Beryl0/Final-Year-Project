Roads, winter weather and local updates

This mock page contains useful public information but uses generic headings and weak image descriptions.

Information

During periods of snow and ice, road teams prioritise key routes and may use gritters or snow clearing equipment. Residents should travel carefully and check service updates before making journeys.

Details

Bin collections can be affected by severe weather. Residents may be asked to leave bins out for collection or return them to their normal storage point depending on the latest update.
